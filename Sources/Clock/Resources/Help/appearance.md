# Appearance and Compact View

The **Appearance** menu selects **Match System**, **Light** or **Dark** for the app's controls and windows. This choice applies to the whole app.

In **Settings → Appearance**, customize the clock background and text colors for the selected clock. These clock-face colors are independent of the appearance of the surrounding controls.

Press **Tab** in a clock window to show or hide its controls. Tab preserves the clock area's size and screen position, including after manual resizing; minimum window sizes still apply. In Settings and duration sheets, Tab moves between fields normally.

In **Settings → Window**, use opacity, always-on-top, borderless and all-Spaces options. Drag the background of a borderless or compact clock to move it. Right-click to restore controls. **Remember window size & position** restores the full window across launches.
